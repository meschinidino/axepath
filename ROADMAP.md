# Axe Path — roadmap

## Where the slice stands: ~75%

The full v1 loop is playable end to end in `Builds/AxePath.app`:
spawn → axe → sketch → uphill forest → wolf → dwarf cold/civil/
sharpening → ironwood → earned edge, with permadeath generations,
title/death/save, HUD, synth audio, night sky, and mountain backdrop.
23 EditMode + 12 PlayMode tests green; viewpoint screenshots verify
every area each run.

Systems are ~90%; content depth, feel, and art are ~50%.

## Next, in order

1. **Notes rework (playtest feedback).** Rewrite blaze/sketch/guide
   texts for voice and usefulness; audit mark placement (visibility
   from the walking line, no floaters, no duplicates); keep the
   books-hint-never-grant rule.
2. **Journey update.** The dwarf path still runs short and easy.
   Lengthen the climb (wind the path, add a second landmark beat),
   tune wolf pressure and dull pacing so arrival feels earned.
3. **Feel and balance.** Chop/wolf-hit feedback juice, swing timing
   vs cooldown, wolf HP/hearing/leash numbers, prompt-hold timings.
   Playtest-driven, one variable at a time.
4. **Small completions.** Pause overlay, volume/mute, save-slot
   labels polish, boot loading indicator, desktop-only notice.
5. **Art milestone** (starts when the loop plays twice with zero
   mechanics complaints). One focused pass, no mechanics changes:
   stylized low-poly pack in the WoW-classic register, engraved
   serif display face (Cinzel or equivalent), composed night music,
   lighting mood pass. Gray-box proportions stay as the spec.

## Post-v1 dreams (unlimited time)

New starting towns, races, roles (mage, fighter…), and stories;
a growing world (towns expand, player housing + decoration);
the BBEG arc (mastery draws unwanted attention); teaching and
legacy (your character dies, the world keeps its changes; killed
NPCs stay dead, knowledge can be lost); multiplayer with shared
permanence; VR support; full body customization and physics.

## Standing rules for this repo

- No tutorials, markers, compass, or quest log. Ever in v1.
- Books and marks hint; skill and trust are earned in the world.
- Dwarf direction: talk through breaking/shared changes first.
- Playtest feel before art; art pass only on a locked loop.
